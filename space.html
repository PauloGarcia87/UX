Case Study

Space

Designing Pricefy Space, a platform that helps retail chains negotiate, track and execute in-store space agreements with suppliers — turning scattered spreadsheets and paper contracts into one clear, auditable workflow.

5
Contracts signed after the project completion
Role
Product Designer (UX/UI)
Tools
Figma, FigJam

Overview

Pricefy Space is a platform that helps retail chains negotiate, track and execute in-store space agreements with suppliers — from shelf placement to seasonal displays. My role was to design the interface that turns hundreds of paper and spreadsheet-based negotiations into a single, auditable workflow for category managers, suppliers and store teams.

Challenge

Category teams were closing hundreds of space agreements a year with almost no shared visibility into what had already been signed, what was expiring, or how much occupancy and revenue each agreement was generating.

  • Contracts lived scattered across spreadsheets and email threads, with no shared record of what had been agreed.
  • Category managers had no easy way to see occupancy rates or compare offers across store types.
  • Store teams often received execution instructions too late to activate new agreements on time.

Process

Exploratory interview with stakeholders to understand how industry negotiations work and ask: what, why, who, when and how.

Desk research to collect data from surveys and interviews previously carried out by Pricefy with the managers.

CSD Workframe were created according to data collected from Zoom to be as assertive as possible when creating the solution.

Exploratory interviews were conducted with 5 interested parties from medium-sized supermarket chains.

Exp. journey map to understand the current process of creating contracts and identify the most critical points.

Decoupage was made analyzing the taped interviews and notes to find patterns of current problems, fears and definition of personas.

Personas

Nara
Category Manager

Negotiates shelf and display space with dozens of suppliers every month and needs one clear place to see what's already been agreed.

Victor
Supplier Account Manager

Represents supplier brands in negotiations and wants transparency on contract status, deadlines and approvals.

Felipe
Store Operations Manager

Executes agreements on the shop floor and needs simple, unambiguous instructions delivered with enough lead time.

Ideate & Prototype

Early concepts were sketched on paper to test occupancy filters, agreement value breakdowns and availability calendars before moving into hi-fi screens.
